# Ads weekly review

Reads first, then proposes. Nothing changes until a person approves the plan in Nexus (or the business lets a tool run by its rule). A temporary stop is **lower bids**, never a pause: an ad serves again about a minute after its bids go back, but only about an hour after a pause is lifted. To stop an Amazon campaign spending for a while, `suppress-campaign` lowers its bids to the 2-cent floor (a suppressed bid is never raised); on eBay, lower the ad rates (2 % is eBay's minimum), the keyword bids or the daily budget. Pause an Amazon ad (`pause-ads`) only when the person says they mean a real pause. Targets per market (target ACoS, ceilings, bid limits) are set with the `ads-strategy` skill; this review measures against them.

## Ground rules

The Nexus server's instructions hold the rules for every change — say which business, read first and ask, one change plan for many changes, follow each change until approval-status says it ran, a temporary ad stop is lower bids (a real pause only when the person means one), never an FBA quantity, never the old flat-file pages. Follow them. Read `business-overview` for the exact market codes and account ids before you name one.

- **Products the ads brain runs (Amazon).** Nexus's ads brain may run a product's levers in a market, each at a level (`OFF`, `OBSERVE` in shadow, `PROPOSE`, `AUTO`). Read `ads-brain` (8) before proposing anything for such a product. Never propose a change on a lever the brain owns on a campaign (its `brain` in the map is `PROPOSE` or `AUTO`, or its `owner` is the brain): no bid, placement, budget, negative, harvest, pause or structure there. Explain what the brain did instead, from its daily product report, and name its requests that wait for the person (a person decides them in Nexus: never ask for them again). Its clashes and tool gaps go in the explanation. Enrolling, a lever's level, a lock, an exclusion and its kill switch are the Owner's own (`set-ads-brain`, `set-brain-kill-switch`): only when he asks for one.
- **When something is missing.** If no Nexus tools are available, Nexus is not connected or its server is off (Claude Code: `/mcp`, choose Nexus, Authenticate; claude.ai: Customize › Connectors). If a tool is not offered, refuses, or says it is turned off for Claude, pass on the reason in plain words and carry on with the rest.

## How to read the numbers

- Amounts are minor units (cents) of each campaign's own currency, with the currency named beside them. Never convert, and never add two markets together: each market is reported on its own.
- The last 3 days are provisional (Amazon restates them for up to 72 hours). `dataAsOf` is the newest day of data.
- A person without the ad-spend money permission sees the same answers without amounts: say so, and review by clicks, orders and ACoS only.
- Live or sandbox is the server's switch: each change preview's `reach` says `live` or `sandbox` (sandbox = recorded in Nexus only). An Amazon sandbox preview skips the live checks (halt, connection, markets, allowlist, pins, spend ceiling, daily budget move, value cap), so it predicts nothing about live.
- Amazon live ad writes happen only in IT, DE, FR and ES; any other Amazon market is refused at the live write gate. On Amazon, `liveWrites` in `ad-campaigns` is the campaign's live-write allowlist: a campaign off it takes no live change, and putting it on (`set-campaign-live-writes`) waits for a person, unless the business lets it run by its rule for a campaign Claude itself created. eBay has no allowlist: its live or sandbox is server-wide (`writes`).

## Amazon's visibility numbers

What Nexus has from Amazon, free:
- **Top-of-search impression share**: per campaign and day; per keyword where Nexus's keyword pass filled it — each reading names its grain. 1–3 days late (the Sponsored Products reports).
- **Brand Analytics Search Query Performance (SQP)**: per search query and ASIN, weekly (Sunday to Saturday), about 5 days after the week ends: our impressions, clicks, cart adds and purchases against the query's totals, and the query's search volume.
- **Best Sellers Rank**: per ASIN and category, every 3 hours (`sales-rank`).

What it does not have: an organic or an ad (sponsored) keyword rank or position. Amazon offers no source for either: never estimate, invent or buy one, and never call a share a rank or a position.

Say each with its grain (campaign, keyword, search query, ASIN), its window (a day, the week of …, 30 days) and its date; "not reported" when there is none, never 0. A value Nexus computes (a weighted average, a sum over ASINs) says "computed by Nexus from Amazon's …"; only a value Amazon reports is Amazon's. A share that moved is explained as a share at its grain and week, never as a rank that moved.

## 1. Per market: read

Ask which channel (`amazon`, the default, `ebay` or `google`) and markets, or take every market with spend. Google Ads is read per account, not per market: see "Google Ads" below. Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) are read per ad account with their own tools: see "Meta ads" below.

1. `ads-overview` (`channel`, `market`, `days`: 7 by default): spend, sales, ACoS, ROAS, against the period before; top spenders; the automation dial; data feed health. eBay: pacing against the monthly ceilings and findings (rates above break-even, missing costs, unpromoted listings). This eBay read also checks the ceilings: a market at its monthly ceiling halts every eBay ad write of the business until a person resumes it — say so if it happens.
2. `ad-campaigns` (`channel`, `market`, `status`, `days`): Amazon per campaign budget, `targetAcos` (a fraction), ACoS, `liveWrites`, suppressed bids, pins. eBay per campaign funding model, campaign rate (`bidPercentage`), daily budget (Priority), `automation` (posture, rules), account; eBay statuses are RUNNING, PAUSED, SYSTEM_PAUSED, ENDED, SUSPENDED, SCHEDULED.
3. Amazon only — `ad-search-terms` (`kind: "wasteful"`, `market`, `days: 30`): terms that spent with no order (negative candidates); a row with `isAsin: true` is an ASIN, negated with `add-negative-targets` (`asins`). The terms that sell come from the harvest engine, the Keyword Harvest page's own: `harvest-candidates` (`market`) — one row per term, product and market, with the bar it passes (`criteria`: the ads strategy's harvest group, else the ads brain's test), its `state` (`READY`, `NEEDS_CAMPAIGN`, `HELD` with `heldBy`, `WAITING` for a person, `HARVESTED` …), where it would go, its funnel blocks and its start bid (an ASIN becomes a product target); `harvest-destinations` (`candidateId`) for one term's places; `harvest-history` (`market`, `days: 30`) for every past harvest of every actor with its verdict after the attribution window + 72 hours (better, worse, too early).
4. Amazon only — `ad-targets` (`campaignId` or `market`, `search`) for the bids behind a problem.
5. `ad-recommendations` (`channel`, `market`, `category`): Amazon — what the engines and the rules suggest, the autopilot plans' waiting decisions (`autopilot`) and the Keyword Tracker's waiting proposals (`tracker`), with the ids each change tool takes and `suggestedTool`. eBay — only the eBay rules' pending proposals (`category` absent or `"rule"`), with no `suggestedTool`. A pause suggestion is information only.
6. `ad-changes` (`channel`, `days: 14`) when the person asks "what changed" or a number moved for no clear reason.
7. Amazon only, when a change touches them: `ad-groups` (`campaignId`: default bids, floors, product ads), `ad-budgets` (`market`: the month's budget plan, budget schedules, pools, baselines), `ad-portfolios` (`market`) and `ad-hourly-plans` (`market`, or `planId` for one plan's week).
8. Amazon only — `ads-brain`: `view: "map"` (`market`) names the products the ads brain runs; for each, `view: "map"` (`productId`, `market`: who owns each lever of each campaign), `view: "report"` (`market`, then `productId`: the brain's daily product report in plain words, what waits for a person) and `view: "clashes"` (`market`); `view: "setup"` for the tools not set up; `view: "money"` (`productId`, `market`) for its off-Amazon lane (`offAmazon`: its share of spend and a line for the Owner when it stays above the band — the setting itself is in Amazon's console, Nexus cannot change it).

`ad-search-terms` and `ad-targets` read Amazon data only: they take no `channel`, and a call with one is refused. eBay: `ebay-ad-details` (`view`: `listings`, `ad-groups` or `keywords`; `campaignId`, `market`, `adGroupId`, `search`, `days`, `limit`, `cursor`) reads each promoted listing's ad rate and break-even rate (`rateNote` says why `set-ebay-ad-rates` cannot change a campaign's rates), a Priority campaign's ad groups, and its keywords with bids and metrics, under the ids `set-ebay-ad-rates` and `ebay-keywords-change` take (`ebayCampaignId`, `ebayItemId`, `ebayAdGroupId`, `ebayKeywordId`). No tool reads eBay search terms. Never call a change tool just to look (it queues a request).

### Google Ads

Nexus reads the Google Ads account a person chose for the business. These numbers come from what Nexus stored (read once
a day); `google-ads-live` reads the account live from Google when a change is planned or checked, and `google-ads-numbers`
reads its numbers live, today included.

- Read `ads-overview` and `ad-campaigns` with `channel: "google"` (`days`; `status`, `search`; `accountId` only when the business has more than one Google account; never `market`). `ad-search-terms`, `ad-targets`, `ad-changes` and `ad-recommendations` do not read Google.
- Then `google-ad-details`, one `view` at a time (`campaignId`, `days`, `search`, `limit`, `cursor`; `accountId` as above):
  - `alerts` first: what needs a look (spend pace, month pace, no conversions, tracking stopped, disapproved ads, products not eligible, limited ads, auto-apply on, changed by Google, settings changed, moved to AI Max). `notChecked` names the checks that could not run and why.
  - `search-terms`: the search terms with at least one click, by cost. Google does not list a search with no click. `brand-split`: cost, clicks and conversions split into brand, not brand and "not in the search-term list", per campaign type. Brand means one of the business's protected terms for every market (`brandTerms`; a person sets them in Nexus, Marketing › Ads › Rules & automation › Control room › Limits). With none set, `brand` is null: say "not marked", never "not brand".
  - `keywords` (by cost), `ad-groups`, `negatives`, `asset-groups` (Performance Max) and `products` (Shopping: the status at Google and why, and the Nexus product with the same SKU).
  - `acts-on-its-own` — what Google acts on by itself: the recommendation types it applies on its own (auto-apply), and per campaign AI Max, broad match, search partners, display expansion, final URL expansion, text customization, dynamic search ads and brand exclusions. A flag that is null is not read yet, never "off". It also lists the changes made outside Nexus and who made them. Changes made with Google Ads Editor never show there.
  - `recommendations`: Google's own, with their expected effect. Say which ones you would follow and why; if the person agrees, `apply-google-recommendations` (or the Google Ads change tool that makes the same change) and `dismiss-google-recommendations` ask for them. Google cannot check an apply or a dismiss first: say so.
  - `disapprovals`: disapproved or limited ads, and products that are not eligible or limited, with Google's reasons.
- A part whose `readAt` is null is not read yet: say "not read yet", never 0.
- The numbers are cost, conversions and conversion value as Google counts them (Google's attribution and conversion actions, days in the account's time zone). Never call them sales, orders or ACoS; return on ad spend is conversion value ÷ cost.
- Conversions can be fractional, and the last 14 days can still rise as Google adds late conversions (`provisionalFrom`). A day with `read: false` has no numbers: say "not read", never 0, and name `coverage.missingDays`. `previous` is there only when Nexus read every day of the window before.
- Changes go through the Google Ads change tools (`writes.mode` approval; every tool, and what Google's API cannot do, in the `google-ads` skill): `set-google-negatives`, `set-google-negative-list`, `set-google-keywords` (bids, bulk bid change), `set-google-status` (pause; enabling is SPENDS MONEY), `set-google-budget`, `set-google-campaign`, `set-google-targeting`, `set-google-product-groups`, `apply-google-recommendations`, … Each is a request a person approves in Nexus, never run by a rule; Google checks most of them first (the preview says when it cannot). Several changes to EXISTING things go in one change plan; new things that point at new things (a campaign and its parts) are one build call (`build-google-campaigns`, `build-google-pmax`). The preview marks what can spend more (SPENDS MONEY): repeat those lines to the person. A temporary stop is lower bids or a lower budget; a pause (`set-google-status`) when the person asks for one.

### Meta ads

Nexus reads the Meta ad account a person chose for the business, with its Facebook Page and Instagram account, per ad account, never per market. The `meta-…` tools read it; they take no `channel`, and `ads-overview`, `ad-campaigns`, `ad-search-terms`, `ad-targets`, `ad-changes` and `ad-recommendations` do not read Meta. Every Meta tool, and what Meta's API cannot do, is in the `meta-ads` skill. With no Meta connection every Meta tool says so: leave Meta out and say why.

Read, in this order:
1. `meta-account-health` (`view: "summary"`: Nexus's own records, no Meta call): the connection and its token, the ad account's status, the daily read. When something does not deliver, `view: "problems"` (live, with Meta's reasons).
2. `meta-ads-overview` (`days: 7`): Nexus's daily read — spend, purchases and purchase value as Meta counts them, ROAS, the window before; then `view: "campaigns"`, `view: "adsets"` and `view: "trend"`.
3. `meta-activity-log` over the week: what changed, who changed it (made outside Nexus too) and why something stopped.
4. `meta-recommendations` (`view: "overview"`): Meta's opportunity score and its recommendations, each with the signature an apply takes.
5. `meta-ad-rules`: Meta's own automated rules, which act inside Meta on their own (trigger rules never show in Ads Manager).
6. `meta-experiments`: the tests that run. Before proposing a change to a campaign, ad set or ad, `view: "object"`: a change can spoil its test.
7. When a question needs it: `meta-ads-numbers` (breakdowns by placement, age, country …; today included), `meta-ad-details` (`view: "diagnostics"`: auction overlap between the account's own ad sets, creative fatigue), `meta-ad-sets` (still learning, where it really runs).

How Meta's numbers differ:
- Purchases and purchase value are Meta's attributed numbers, counted with each ad set's own attribution setting. Never call them sales, orders or ACoS, and never add them to Amazon's or Google's; ROAS is purchase value ÷ spend, as Meta counts them. Amounts are minor units of the ad account's currency.
- The last 28 days of the daily read are provisional (Meta still adds late purchases). A day Nexus did not read is "not read", never 0; the window before is shown only when both were read in full.
- A breakdown switched off for the ad account answers nothing: say "switched off or no delivery", never 0. Audience sizes and reach are Meta's ranges and estimates, never one exact number.

What to propose, with the Meta change tools, as steps of the review's one plan: a pause (`set-meta-status`) or a lower bid or budget (`set-meta-ad-set`, `set-meta-campaign`) where Meta spends with no purchase; budget moved toward what sells (a raise is SPENDS MONEY); targeting or placements (`set-meta-targeting`, `set-meta-placements`); the recommendations the person agrees with (`apply-meta-recommendations`; a dismiss is Ads Manager only). Each waits for a person — or runs after the short window when the business switched "Claude's changes need no approval" on — and never by a rule of its own. Meta has no ads strategy in Nexus: judge against the numbers the person gives, never targets of your own.

Never: ask to switch on anything (an ad, an ad set, a campaign, a Meta rule) the person did not ask for; delete what they did not ask to delete (`confirmDelete` only on their word); make or switch on a Meta automated rule unless they ask (it then acts inside Meta on its own); switch an Advantage+ creative feature on unless they name it; add people to a customer list (Nexus adds nobody: its customers carry no marketing consent yet). New campaigns, ad sets and ads are born paused and asked for one after another, never as steps of one plan.

## 2. Explain

Per market, a few lines: spend and sales against last week, ACoS against the target the business holds in Nexus (`ads-strategy`), the 3 biggest wastes, the 3 best terms ready to harvest and the verdicts of past harvests (Amazon: `harvest-candidates`, `harvest-history`), campaigns over budget or starved, anything an engine flags. For each product the ads brain runs: what it did or would do in shadow, what waits for a person, its clashes and the tools not set up. Then a short list of proposed changes with why — none on a lever the brain owns.

Google Ads, per account: cost, conversions and conversion value against the period before (only when `previous` is there), the alerts, the brand split, the search terms that cost most with no conversion, what Google did on its own, and the Google Ads changes you would ask for and why.

Meta ads, per ad account: spend, purchases and purchase value as Meta counts them against the window before (only when both were read in full), the account's problems, the campaigns and ad sets that spend with no purchase, what changed outside Nexus, what Meta's rules and tests are doing, and the Meta changes you would ask for and why.

## 4. After

`approval-status` shows `ads` (Amazon writes waiting, sent, refused by the write gate, failed, or run in sandbox) and `ebay` for eBay writes (sent, sandbox, partly, failed). A Google Ads change shows in `google-ads-live` at once, and in Nexus's stored Google views after its next read. A Meta change shows in `meta-ads-live` at once, and in `meta-ads-overview` after Nexus's next daily read. A harvest that ran shows in `harvest-history` with its keyword and blocks; its verdict comes after the attribution window + 72 hours. `undo-change` asks to put an approved change back (see `review-and-undo`); on eBay an undo never removes anything: promoted listings drop to the 2 % rate, added keywords to a 2-cent bid, added negatives stay, and a created campaign stays.
