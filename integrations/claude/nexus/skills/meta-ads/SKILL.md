# Meta ads

Nexus works in the Meta ad account, Facebook Page and Instagram account that a person chose for the business. Every change is a request with a preview; it runs only after a person's yes, as that person. Amounts are minor units (cents) of the ad account's currency (a currency with no decimals has whole units): never convert them.

## Ground rules

The Nexus server's instructions hold the rules for every change — say which business, read first and ask, one change plan for many changes, follow each change until approval-status says it ran, never an FBA quantity, never the old flat-file pages. Follow them. Read `business-overview` before you name an account.

- **No Meta keys, no Meta.** Meta works only after a person connected it for this business in Nexus (Settings › Channels › Meta: the business's Meta app and a system-user token). Until then every Meta tool refuses in words ("Meta is not connected for this business"): pass that on and leave the Meta part. Never take an app secret or a token in the chat. Nexus renews the token itself.
- **When something is missing.** If no Nexus tools are available, Nexus is not connected or its server is off (Claude Code: `/mcp`, choose Nexus, Authenticate; claude.ai: Customize › Connectors). If a tool is not offered, refuses, or says it is turned off for Claude, pass on the reason in plain words and carry on with the rest.

## Safety rules

1. **Preview, a person's yes, then it runs as that person.** A Meta change tool changes nothing by itself: it returns a preview and an approval id. Nothing changes until a person approves it in Nexus (Approvals). When the business has switched on "Claude's changes need no approval", it runs about 20 seconds after the request unless someone stops it (over a connection allowed to run changes, while Claude is not paused; the answer then says `runs_by_rule`). Either way it runs as that person, never by a rule of its own. Claude's levels and the ads strategy never let a Meta change run alone; a business can only turn a Meta tool off for Claude. When the preview says "Meta ads writes are switched off in Nexus", tell the person that nothing reaches Meta after their approval.
2. **Born paused.** New campaigns, ad sets, ads and copies are made PAUSED, except what Meta makes when a recommendation is applied with `allowNewObjects: true` (it may start running: SPENDS MONEY). `activate: true` makes one ACTIVE: SPENDS MONEY, the person's decision. A new Meta automated rule is made OFF unless `enable: true`. Switching on later is `set-meta-status`.
3. **Advantage+ stays off unless asked.** Meta can switch creative features on by itself (generated text, image and video changes, music, format changes and more). Nexus sends every Advantage+ creative feature OFF (OPT_OUT) unless the person names it in `aiFeatures`; multi-advertiser ads, music, format automation, shop routing and automatic product tags are off unless asked; an ad set's Advantage+ audience is sent off unless asked. An ad goes ACTIVE (`create-meta-ad` with `activate: true`, or a new creative swapped onto an ACTIVE ad with `set-meta-ad`) only when its creative reads OPT_OUT for EVERY Advantage+ feature Meta documents. A feature on, or one Nexus cannot read: make the ad PAUSED, preview it (`meta-ad-preview`), then a person switches it on with `set-meta-status`.
4. **SPENDS MONEY is the person's decision.** The preview marks every way a change can make the account spend more: switching on; a budget, bid or spend cap raised or removed; an account spending limit raised, removed or reset; a lower ROAS goal, a longer run, accelerated pacing; wider targeting or more placements on an ad set that is on; looser account controls or brand safety; a scheduled budget increase; a reach and frequency booking; a Meta rule that can raise spend switched on, made on or run, and a rule that pauses or cuts switched off, changed or deleted; a recommendation that raises a budget or makes new ads; a change of who is in an audience that ads use; a pixel connected again or its events given back to ads; a pixel connected to a catalog, a new default or fallback image, or a product set grown, while ads deliver on it; purchases sent to Meta; a published Instant Experience shown again (an ad that links to it may show it again). The line says when a parent is off, so nothing spends yet. Repeat each line to the person before they decide. Never ask to switch on anything they did not ask for.
5. **A stop is a pause or a lower bid or budget** (`set-meta-status` PAUSED; a lower bid or budget with `set-meta-ad-set` or `set-meta-campaign`). Archive (`set-meta-status` ARCHIVED) only what is meant for good: Meta lets an archived campaign, ad set or ad only be deleted, never switched on again.
6. **Delete only on the person's explicit ask.** Every delete needs `confirmDelete: true`, set only when the person said to delete — never for "stop": `remove-meta-ads-items` (a campaign takes its ad sets and ads with it; the preview lists them), `remove-meta-audience`, `remove-meta-ad-rule`, `remove-meta-block-list`, `remove-meta-alert-subscription`, `remove-meta-signal-items`, `remove-meta-catalog-items` (a catalog, product set, items or a supplementary feed; the preview lists what stops), a comment (`set-meta-comments`, `action: "delete"`; hide it instead when the person means "out of sight"), and the delete of `set-meta-labels`, `set-meta-value-rules` and `set-meta-lead-form` (a test lead). Four deletes take no `confirmDelete` flag: ask for them, too, only on the person's explicit ask — a scheduled budget increase (`set-meta-budget-schedule`, `action: "delete"`), a concluded Page video test (`set-meta-experiment`, `action: "delete-page-video-test"`), a partnership ad code of the chosen Instagram account's own media (`set-meta-partnership-access`, `action: "delete-ad-code"`; no undo: Meta cannot bring it back) and Nexus's earlier test lead of a form (`set-meta-lead-form` `test-lead` with `replaceTestLead`). Meta deletes no Instant Experience: hide it instead (`set-meta-instant-experience`, `action: "hide"`). Publishing one is for good too — it can never be edited again — so `confirmPublish: true` only once the person knows that.
7. **Customer lists add nobody.** Nexus uploads only customers who gave marketing consent, and its customer records hold no consent yet: a customer list is made EMPTY, and `set-meta-audience-members` refuses to add or replace people. It only takes people who opted out of the business's e-mail out of the lists. Making an audience, and taking people out of a list, are refused until a person signs Meta's Custom Audience terms (`meta-audiences` `view: "terms"` says whether they are signed).
8. **Sending sales to Meta is off by default.** `upload-meta-events` (the Conversions API) works only after a person turned "Send sales to Meta" on for this business (Settings › Channels › Meta); Claude cannot. It never sends the shop's website sales (the shop's own pixel sends them: a copy would count twice) nor marketplace sales; only orders taken in by hand in Nexus, and Nexus has no way to make such an order yet, so nothing is sent today. Read `meta-datasets` `view: "sources"` first. Sent purchases cannot be taken back.
9. **This business's Meta ids only.** Nexus reads and changes only the ad account, Page and Instagram account chosen for this business. Each Meta id (ad account, Page, Instagram account, pixel, catalog, audience) belongs to ONE business in Nexus: one another business uses is refused, or shown by its id only. Never carry a Meta id from another connection.
10. **Step by step.** Meta has no all-or-nothing request. A change sends its requests one by one, in order, and the answer says what Meta did at each step; what ran before a failure stays done. A change sent to Meta has no cancel window. Meta takes a change of the same object only 30 seconds after the last one, and an ad set's budget at most 4 times an hour (its undo counts): keep each plan small.
11. **Meta's check only where it is proven.** Meta checks a request before it is queued (validate_only) only where Nexus knows Meta honours it: a campaign pause, and the create of a campaign, an ad set, a creative (a boost too) or an ad. For every other change the preview says Meta has no check (or not yet), and the run reads the live state again before it sends. Tell the person which kind they approve.
12. **No political or social-issue ads** (banned in the EU; a special ad category is never political). An ad set that may reach the EU needs a payer and a beneficiary: the ad account's saved defaults (`meta-ad-account` `view: "dsa"`), else given in the request — refused in words with neither.

## 1. Which business, which Meta account

1. `business-overview`: the business. With several Nexus connections, `nexus-business-check` first.
2. `meta-connection`, `view: "summary"` (no Meta call): the system user, the token's health, expiry and missing permissions, the chosen ad account, Page and Instagram account, the API version. `view: "check"` asks Meta now (usage, access tier).
3. `meta-ad-accounts`: the chosen ad account and the portfolio's others (one another business chose shows its id only). Another ad account, Page or Instagram account: `set-meta-connection`, from `meta-connection` `view: "choices"` (saved in Nexus only; a person approves it).
4. `meta-account-health`: `view: "summary"` (Nexus's own records, no Meta call), then live `account`, `problems` (one sweep of everything that does not deliver, with Meta's reasons), `payment`, `signals`, `page`, `connection`. Meta fixes none of these by API: appeals and payments are a person's, in Meta's screens.

## 2. Read first

Nexus's daily read costs no Meta call. The live reads ask Meta now and count against the account's Meta limits: use them to plan a change and to check one after it ran. Read one `view` at a time.

- **Numbers:** `meta-ads-overview` (the daily read: the account, campaigns, ad sets, ads, days, weekly trend); `meta-ads-live` (this minute, numbers inline); `meta-ads-numbers` (any metric, breakdown, window and period; `view: "catalogue"` lists them); `meta-ads-report` (a big read in the background; starting one changes nothing); `meta-ads-saved-reports` (Ads Reporting and partner reports, read only); `meta-ads-graph-read` (one allow-listed GET for what nothing else reads; prefer the others); `detect-anomalies` (`channel: "meta"`: unusual swings, computed from the daily read, and what Meta itself pushed).
- **Account:** `meta-ad-account` (settings, EU payer and beneficiary, terms, account controls, minimum budgets, which breakdowns are switched on), `meta-billing` (read only), `meta-account-access`, `meta-activity-log` (the change history; changes made outside Nexus; why something stopped), `ad-changes` (`channel: "meta"`: the changes made through Nexus, who decided each, whether undo can still put it back).
- **Campaigns, ad sets, ads:** `meta-campaigns`, `meta-ad-sets` (learning, where it really runs, delivery issues), `meta-ad-details` (ads and creatives, the Advantage+ features Meta really applied, problems, auction overlap and creative fatigue), `meta-labels`, `meta-value-rules`, `meta-ad-preview` (a preview link is public for 24 hours: give it to the person only, never store it).
- **Targeting:** `meta-targeting-search` (the ids of places, interests, behaviours and more), `meta-targeting-check` (still valid or retiring, reach as a range, who sees it in Meta's words).
- **Media and posts:** `meta-media-library`, `meta-page-posts`, `meta-instagram-media` (what can be boosted and why not), `meta-instant-experiences`.
- **Audiences and pixels:** `meta-audiences` (read `view: "ads-using"` before deleting, unsharing or changing one), `meta-datasets` (pixels; whether the shop already sends purchases by server; event match quality; custom conversions).
- **Catalogs and shops:** `meta-catalogs` (`view: "writer"`: who writes the items — where Shopify feeds a catalog, Shopify keeps writing and Nexus writes no items), `meta-catalog-items`, `meta-catalog-feeds`, `meta-catalog-health`, `meta-product-sets`, `meta-commerce`, `meta-instagram-product-tags`.
- **Leads and comments:** `meta-lead-forms`, `meta-leads`, `meta-comments`. Leads and comments carry people's names, e-mails and words: use them only for what the person asked; Nexus keeps none. Real leads need Meta's App Review first: until then Meta answers test leads only.
- **Partners and research:** `meta-partnership-content`, `meta-ad-library`.
- **Brand safety:** `meta-brand-safety`. Block lists and delivery reports need Meta's grant: without it the answer names the controls that need none.
- **Webhooks and alerts:** `meta-webhooks`.
- **What Meta does on its own:** `meta-recommendations` (the opportunity score; each recommendation with the signature an apply takes), `meta-experiments` (`view: "object"`: is a campaign, ad set or ad in a test — a change can spoil it), `meta-ad-rules` (Meta's own automated rules, trigger rules too, which never show in Ads Manager).

## 3. How to read Meta's numbers

- Purchases and purchase value are Meta's attributed numbers, counted with each ad set's own attribution setting (as Ads Manager does). They are not Nexus orders: never call them sales or orders. ROAS is purchase value ÷ spend, as Meta counts them. 7-day and 28-day view windows answer nothing since 2026-01-12.
- In the daily read the last 28 days are provisional (Meta still adds late purchases). A day Nexus did not read is "not read", never 0. The period before is shown only when both were read in full.
- A breakdown can be switched off for the ad account; Meta then answers nothing, without an error. Say "switched off or no delivery", never 0 (`meta-ad-account` `view: "insights-features"` says which are on).
- Every breakdown value is Meta's estimate, and so are reach and the other numbers Meta marks as estimated (`meta-ads-numbers` `view: "catalogue"` says which). Audience sizes and reach are ranges, never one number; Meta gives no size for a lookalike.
- A comparison with an earlier period is computed by Nexus (Meta's insights take none). Industry benchmarks have no public API: Meta's own MCP or Ads Manager.
- Amounts are minor units of the ad account's currency; `meta-ads-graph-read` and fields named `…AsMeta` are in Meta's own units. A person without the ad-spend money permission gets the same answers without amounts, and `meta-billing` and `set-meta-spend-cap` are refused (not offered): say so.

## 4. Plan, then ask

Show one table: what changes, from → to, how many, the SPENDS MONEY lines, what cannot be undone, and whether Meta checks it first. Name the business and the ad account. Ask, and go on only after a clear yes.

**Learning.** Before a change to a running ad set, read `meta-ad-sets` (`view: "adset"`: still learning or not; `view: "delivery-stats"`: its learning stage). A budget change, a new bid strategy, ROAS goal, performance goal or pixel event, a targeting or creative change, and taking people out of an audience it uses can start Meta's learning again; targeting, placement and creative changes, and a new performance goal, also send its ads back to review. Repeat the preview's warnings with its SPENDS MONEY lines. Change budgets and bids at most 2–3 times a day, early in the day, one change at a time; do not judge or change an ad set still learning unless the person asks.

- **Changes to things that exist:** ONE `submit-change-plan`, one step per tool (see `change-plan`). Each step is checked now against Meta's live state, as if asked alone, so steps cannot wait for each other.
- **New things that point at new things** (a campaign, its ad set, its ad): Nexus has no one-call Meta build. Ask for them one at a time, each once the one before ran (`approval-status`; then read its new id with `meta-campaigns` or `meta-ad-sets`): `create-meta-campaign`, then `create-meta-ad-set`, then the media (`create-meta-media`) and the creative (`create-meta-creative`, or `create-meta-boost` for an existing post), then `create-meta-ad`. Each is born paused. To repeat what works, `copy-meta-ads`: the copy stays in the chosen ad account (a campaign, an ad set into another of its campaigns, an ad into another of its ad sets), born paused.
- Give `why` on every change: the approver reads it.

## 6. What Meta's API cannot do

Say so plainly, and that the person does it in Meta's screens:
- **Money and the account:** payment methods, prepaid funds, card receipts and credit lines (Meta Business settings); the account's currency or time zone (a new ad account is the way); appeals and Account Quality (Meta Business Suite).
- **Terms and access:** signing the Custom Audience terms (Ads Manager); business verification, App Review and the higher access tier.
- **Ads Manager only:** dismissing a recommendation; making or publishing drafts (Nexus reads them); saved reports, custom metrics and columns (Ads Manager › Reports); results per finished dynamic-creative combination; saved audiences.
- **Not by API:** switching a breakdown off again (Meta has no way by API, nor seen in Meta's screens); deleting a study or a pixel; editing or deleting a lead form (Nexus archives it; Meta's Forms library holds it); deleting an Instant Experience or editing a published one (Nexus hides it; a new one is the way); a custom conversion's rule never changes once made (a new one is the way).
- **Events Manager:** event rules and parameter extractors (Nexus only reads them); the code for Meta's test events (Events Manager › Test events; `testEventCode` is not a dry run: Meta counts test events).
- **Elsewhere:** Instagram and WhatsApp webhook fields (the App Dashboard); commerce settings (Commerce Manager); removing a product tag from a post (the Instagram app); a partnership ad code (the Instagram app: Nexus cannot show it later); real leads before App Review (Meta's Leads Center); a video Meta will not take from the system user (the Owner uploads it in Meta).
- **Never:** political or social-issue ads in the EU. A legacy Advantage+ shopping campaign: Meta's API refuses every edit of it in v26 (most since v25), and its migration too; a person migrates it in Ads Manager (one with more than 50 ads in an ad set cannot be migrated). Industry benchmarks: Meta's own MCP or Ads Manager.

## 7. Coming, not in Nexus yet

Say "not in Nexus yet" and name the place in Meta: making or changing catalog items and feeds (Shopify writes the items: change or remove them in Shopify; `remove-meta-catalog-items` deletes items and supplementary feeds only in a catalog Shopify does not feed), Page and Instagram posts, Messenger and Instagram Direct messages, people and partner access to the ad account and the Page, and the business portfolio's admin.

## 8. After, and undo

- `approval-status` with the `approvalId`: never say a change ran before it says so. Then read the object again: `meta-ads-live` (or the read of that object) shows what Meta holds at once; Nexus's stored views after its next daily read.
- `undo-change` asks for the opposite change, a new request a person approves (see `review-and-undo`). It reads the object live first and puts back only what the change wrote. A new campaign, ad set, ad or copy is deleted by its undo only while nothing hangs under it and it spent nothing; an ad that ran stays (archive it). Never undone: a delete (except a scheduled budget increase's, while it has not started), an archive, an Instant Experience's publish, a copy made in the background, a scheduled budget increase, a new pixel, a new study, a rule's run, people taken out of a customer list, purchases sent. A lead form's undo archives it; a new Instant Experience's undo hides it (its parts stay on the Page). Each tool's description says what its undo does.
