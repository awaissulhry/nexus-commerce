# 04 — Fact-check of the marketplace rules shown in the Nexus Reviews design

Checked 2026-10-26, from official sources only: developer docs, API models, seller help pages and policy pages. I did not log in anywhere.

Two sources need a note:
- **Amazon Seller Central help pages.** Their text was read through the public help-hub content service, `POST /help/hub/mons-api/GetHelpTopic`, the same call the public page makes. The human-readable URL is listed.
- **Seller Forums post by Josh_Amazon.** Josh_Amazon appears to be Amazon staff, but the page does not say so. Where this post is the only source, the verdict says so.

## UI text to change

1. **Amazon review request:**
   - Say: "Amazon sends one standard email, translated into the buyer's language, asking for a product review and seller feedback. Allowed once per order, 5–30 days after delivery."
   - Add: "Sending it uses the order's one review request. After that, no review request may go by Buyer-Seller Messaging."
   - Don't list supported countries. Show "available" only when Amazon reports the order eligible (`getSolicitationActionsForOrder`).
2. **Amazon review insights (Customer Feedback API):**
   - Remove any star breakdown, average or count. Show topics instead: mentions, % of reviews, effect on stars (±), English snippets, 6-month trend. "Updated weekly."
   - Markets: UK, DE, FR, IT, ES. Say "Not available on Amazon.nl."
   - Requirement: "Needs the Selling Partner Insights role". Don't say "brand owners only".
3. **Amazon seller feedback import:** "Amazon's feedback report has only 1–3 star feedback and is fetched on request (it can't be scheduled)." Don't promise 4–5 star seller feedback from this source.
4. **Amazon feedback removal:**
   - "You can ask Amazon to remove feedback that is obscene, contains personal details, or is entirely a product review — within 90 days, in Feedback Manager."
   - "Complaints only about FBA delivery (or a late order you shipped on time with Buy Shipping) are struck through, not removed."
   - Mark the 90-day limit "per Amazon staff guidance".
5. **Amazon "ask buyer to remove negative feedback" action:** Remove it, or show it only if Amazon still lists `negativeFeedbackRemoval` for the order. It is no longer in Amazon's official Messaging API.
6. **Amazon reply to feedback:** "Reply in Seller Central (Feedback Manager). Public. One reply. Can't be edited; if you delete it you can't reply again." No "Reply from Nexus" button for Amazon.
7. **Package inserts:**
   - Allowed: "Thank you for your purchase! Feel free to share your honest feedback by leaving a review!"
   - Not allowed: asking for positive or 5-star reviews, star graphics, gifts or discounts, QR codes to outside sites, contact details, or "contact us first if you're unhappy".
8. **Review gating:** "Ask every buyer the same way. Asking only happy buyers, or sending unhappy buyers elsewhere, is not allowed."
9. **eBay reply:** "One reply per feedback, up to 500 characters, can't be edited or removed." Replace every "80 characters" with 500. Follow-up: "One follow-up on feedback you left for a buyer."
10. **eBay leave feedback for buyers:**
    - "Positive only, up to 500 characters, within 90 days of the completed sale."
    - Photos: say "Buyers can add up to 5 photos." Don't promise photos on seller→buyer feedback.
11. **eBay revision requests:**
    - "5 per calendar year, plus 5 for every 1,000 feedback received that year. Only for feedback under 30 days old. Once per feedback. The buyer has 10 days."
    - Show "Resolve the problem first" as advice, not a rule.
12. **eBay channel:** No country limit for IT and DE. The Feedback API works per eBay account. No change needed beyond dropping any country caveat.
13. **Etsy reply:**
    - "Reply on Etsy.com. One public reply, within 100 days of the buyer's last edit. Can't be edited (only deleted, no second reply). After you reply the buyer can no longer change the review."
    - No "Reply from Nexus" button for Etsy: the API has none.
14. **Etsy review data:** One photo per review at most. No video, sub-ratings or seller reply via API. Buyer name and order link only from the shop-level call, and may be hidden for privacy.
15. **Shopify review apps:**
    - Judge.me: "Read and reply" (public and private reply).
    - Okendo: "Read and reply (Okendo Enterprise only)".
    - Yotpo: "Read only — reply in Yotpo".
    - Loox: "Read only (Convert/Unlimited plan) — reply in Loox".
