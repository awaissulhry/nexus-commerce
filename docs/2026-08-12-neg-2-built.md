## 1 · The join, and why it is the whole section

`AmazonAdsSearchTerm.adGroupId` is an **external Amazon id**. `AdTarget` reaches its ad group
through a **local cuid** and carries the external id one hop away at `adGroup.externalAdGroupId`.
Join the wrong pair and `overlap` is 0 for every term in the account, forever, and it reads exactly
like good news.

I computed **both** joins before writing the route (`scripts/_neg2-probe.mts`):

| term | overlap (external↔external) | the WRONG join (external↔local) |
|---|---|---|
| `giacca moto` | 0 | 0 |
| **`saponette moto`** | **1** | **0** |
| `xavia` | 0 | 0 |

The wrong join returns 0 for **every** term including the only true live conflict in the account.
So `saponette moto` is the only fixture in the repo that can tell the two apart, and
`_neg2-termcontext.mts` asserts it — plus re-derives the same number straight from the two tables,
so a bug in the service cannot agree with itself.

**Reconciled against the study's own oracle** (`_neg-page-conflict.mts` §A, re-run today):

| term | oracle negIn / runsIn / overlap | route |
|---|---|---|
| `giacca moto` | 48 +2c / 5 / 0 | 48 / 5 / 0 ✓ |
| `saponette moto` | 4 / 5 / **1** | 4 / 5 / **1** ✓ |
| `giacca moto estiva uomo` | 17 +1c / 8 / 0 | 17 / 8 / 0 ✓ |

No disagreement. The oracle's own `negIn … +Nc` presentation is the same two-number split the
drawer renders, which is a good sign the split is the data's shape and not mine.

*(Its absolute totals have moved since the study — 185 `(query, adGroup)` rows against 188, €2,657.60
against €2,834.76 — because the 30-day window rolls. The relationships are unchanged.)*

One thing checked before trusting the oracle at all: **all 5,383 distinct `query` values over 120
days are already normalised**, so its exact `in` match is not silently under-counting.

## 2 · 🔴 The number the brief did not ask for

`overlap.length` is **not** the number of writes it would take to clear one.

`saponette moto` overlaps in **one** ad group — which holds **two** negation rows for the term,
`_PHRASE` and `_EXACT`. An ad group can negate the same term at more than one match type. That is
the term/negation grain split appearing one level further down, and reporting "1" over two writes is
exactly the defect class this page exists to stop.

I found it because an assertion failed, and **the assertion was wrong, not the code**. The payload
now returns both `overlap` (ad groups) and `overlapRows` (writes), the drawer says *"2 negations to
clear it"*, and **NEG.3's confirm must use `overlapRows`.**

Same shape one level up, and also returned as two numbers rather than one:

- `spread.adGroups` = **49** for `giacca moto` — local ids over every negation. This is the grid
  chip's number, so the chip you clicked and the drawer it opened cannot disagree.
- `comparable.negatedAdGroups` = **48** — external ids, ad-group-scoped rows only. This is the set
  traffic can actually be compared against.

They differ by the campaign-level rows, whose ad group is an FK convenience and not a place Amazon
blocks anything — and all 22 of those in the account carry no Amazon id at all. The drawer says so
in a clause rather than leaving two "ad groups" numbers to be reconciled by the reader.

## 3 · What shipped

**One route, four consumers.** `GET /advertising/negatives/term-context?term=…&market=…&window=…`
plus the four scope grains. NEG.3's confirm needs `performance` and `remainder`; NEG.4's detectors
need `overlap` and `history`; the Automations inbox card needs the same summary. **They consume this
and must not re-derive it** — three derivations would disagree, and the one that disagreed would be
the one on the confirm dialog.

🔴 **Facts, not verdicts.** There is no `isConflict` boolean and there should not be one: whether an
overlap is a conflict, or a dark 120-day earner is a suppressed earner, is a threshold decision that
belongs to NEG.4. Hard-coding a policy here would force that section to fight this one.

**The drawer**, six blocks: header + spread + protection badge · the three numbers · what it earns ·
where it still runs · every negation · the remainder sentence.

**The three numbers stay three.** `negated in` / `runs in` / `overlap`, with the sentence underneath
saying what an overlap of 0 actually means — every ad group that negates the term is a different one
from every ad group taking its traffic, so the negative is **routing** the term rather than blocking
it. No euro figure is called "lost revenue" anywhere.

**Entry points:** the term cell in both grids, and the spread chip — which used to answer "where else
is this?" by pushing `{view:'terms', q}`, i.e. with a filtered grid. `?focus=` deep-links it. Escape,
the backdrop and the close button all dismiss through `push`, so the URL stays the single writer of
page state. `NegWriteActions.onRowAction` is untouched: opening a drawer is a read.

**The first column becomes interactive**, so NEG.1's override of the shared grid's blue stays and
gains a hover underline instead. The colour said "not a link" when it was not one; the underline says
"opens something" now that it is.

### 🔴 A fifth empty state the deploy split forces

The brief names four (read failed · never negated · negated but silent · no search-term data at all).
There is a fifth, and it is invisible until you look for it: **our 404 and Fastify's route-missing
404 are both 404.**

A commit is two deploys. In the window between the web deploy and the API deploy, every term would
have reported *"nothing negates this term"* — false, and reassuring. The client discriminates on
`code: 'term_not_negated'`, never on the status, and says "this view is not available yet" otherwise.

## 4 · Verified on production, by measurement

- **Geometry.** Backdrop covers the full viewport (0,0 → 2076×817, z 1200); the panel is anchored to
  the viewport's right edge (720px, z 1201) and scrolls internally. `scrollWidth === clientWidth`.
- 🔴 **The containing-block trap is absent.** A `position: fixed` panel is clipped or offset by any
  ancestor with `transform` / `filter` / `perspective` / `will-change` / `contain`. Walked the whole
  ancestor chain on prod: **zero offenders**.
- **Contrast**: every probed pair ≥ 4.5:1. Lowest is the performance-tile label at **4.54** (10px,
  700, `#667080` on `#f1f4f8`) — passes AA, with less margin than the rest of the page.
- **Interactions**, all five: deep link opens · Escape closes · term cell opens · backdrop closes ·
  spread chip opens (and no longer switches the grid to the terms view) · close button closes. Every
  one leaves the URL correct.
- **The remainder sentence changes with the scope.** Account-wide: *"Every negation of this term is
  listed above. Your scope is the whole account, so there is no remainder"* — stated, not left to
  silence. Scoped to one campaign: *"1 of 72 negations are in your scope. Removing it would leave
  **71 still blocking this term in 40 campaigns** outside it."* All 72 stay listed — **scope
  highlights, it never filters** — with in-scope rows first and visually distinct.
- **`xavia`** renders the protection badge with the CONTAINS semantics and its reason, and the clause
  that matters: *"The negations below predate that rule; the protection stops new ones, it does not
  remove these."* ACoS renders **—**, not 0%.
- **Zero console messages.**

Two things I checked that turned out to be my error, not the page's: in-scope rows *do* sort first
(only one row was in scope, so my "first three" check was meaningless), and the count chip and its
sub-label are separated by a real 8px gap (`textContent` concatenation made them look glued —
measuring the rects is the only way to tell).
