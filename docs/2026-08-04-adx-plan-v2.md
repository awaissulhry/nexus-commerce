## Part 3 — The findings that shape this plan

**Four independent arrivals — treat as settled:**
> **Bounds and provenance are the price of admission.** Scale Insights (200+ parameters, "Absurd Control"), M19 (opaque ML, five dials), Teikametrics (`ACOS Limit` + `Bid Modifier` around a proprietary model) and your desktop study's Tier-1 #4 all require the same two things: the operator must be able to **bound** it and **see why**. Neither pole ships an unbounded optimiser.

**Seven independent arrivals:**
> **Commerce state should gate ad spend.** 5 of 5 in your study; reached twice more here. `NEXUS_ADS_RETAIL_GUARD_APPLY` being absent from Railway is the best-supported single finding across both studies.

**Three independent arrivals:**
> **One number, priced in €.** Your "counted problems board priced in €", Pacvue's dollar-valued alerts, BidX's Wasted Ad Spend Analyzer.

**Design constraints adopted from Quartile's failure modes:**
1. Automation must leave behind a structure a human could still run by hand.
2. Anything that *creates* entities needs a *retirement* path designed at the same time.
3. If automation makes the account too big to see, the tool owes you a way to see it.

**The calibration:** BidX prices its payback at **€10k/month ad spend**. Xavia runs **~€4.5k**. The winning system here is *small, legible and cheap to operate* — not feature-complete. Scale Insights' 1%-of-spend plan (~€45/mo) is the only tool in the study priced for this volume, and it beats us today only because our engine had never run.

**The honest limitation:** every competitor measures page-one ownership by **scraping Amazon**, which its ToS prohibits. We will approximate instead (Part 4, C2). Our coverage numbers will be weaker than a Pacvue dashboard and **not directly comparable**. Stated up front rather than discovered later.

## Part 4 — The phases

Four stages. Each phase separately gated; nothing starts without your go.

### Stage A — Make it trustworthy *(prerequisites; nothing else is safe first)*

**A1 · Bounds on the entity + keyword protection** 🔴 *closes a live exposure*
- `minBid` / `maxBid` / `targetAcos` as **columns** on campaign and portfolio; `minPrice` / `maxPrice` / cost floor on product. Not an exemption table — *a column cannot be bypassed by a future engine that forgets to check it.*
- **Keyword whitelist / blacklist** (Scale Insights). Your `🌾 Auto harvest & negate` rule is enabled **right now with nothing protecting a brand term from being negated.** This is the one item that is an exposure rather than a build.
- Enforced at `ads-write-gate.ts` — the existing single chokepoint.

**A2 · Provenance + the one number**
- Every writer records its reasoning in the same transaction: engine, rule/schedule/window, evidence (metric, threshold, window, sample size), before→after.
- Close the four blind spots — `ad-rank-defend`, `ad-dayparting`, `ads-top-of-search`, `rank-controller` currently write **zero** `AdvertisingActionLog` rows. Plus 10,348 null-attributed rows.
- Unified change history, filterable by source / entity / field.
- **A single "wasted ad spend, €" figure** from `ProductProfitDaily` + real fees — margin actually lost, not an ACOS estimate.

**A3 · Commerce state gates ad spend** *(mostly flags, not build)*
- Verify the retail guard's proposals, then set `NEXUS_ADS_RETAIL_GUARD_APPLY`.
- Verify budget enforcement, then set `NEXUS_BUDGET_ENFORCE_APPLY`.
- Extend gate → **allocator** (Rithum): rank advertisable products by margin × stock cover × Buy Box and let budget follow the ranking. Lands on the dormant APS series.

### Stage B — Make it controllable

**B1 · Control Console + Foresight**
- `/marketing/ads/autopilot` (existing "AI Control" rail entry) becomes tabs: `Autonomy · Levers · Guardrails · Activity`.
- Every lever one row: mode (**Off / Observe / Propose / Auto**), bounds, scope, accountability strip (applied this week · last run · enabled since · net effect), revert.
- Wire the **existing** `autonomy/status` · `pause-all` · `resume` · `automation/state` endpoints, plus write-gate controls and manual cron triggers.
- **Preview before execution + step-by-step calculation** — Scale Insights' differentiator.
- **Next-24-hours across all engines**; **30-day dry-run diff** before enabling any lever.
- **7-day undo** (Prism). **Per-category thresholds** — auto for bids/negatives/budget within bounds; **structural change always gated** (industry consensus).

### Stage C — Make it work toward the goal

**C1 · Rank-engine coverage extension** *(goals-first is incomplete without this)*
- Today the rank engine covers **33 of 82 enabled campaigns — 49% of spend.** Disabling the rank *rules* lost nothing (they never ran), but it did not close that gap.
- Extend via `RankScheduleGroup` — 16 exist, only 4 enabled, 3 portfolio-scoped. Built for exactly this.
- **Goal categories as the authoring entry point**: **Defend · Expand · Profit · Suppress** (Perpetua's intents + Pacvue's defense/expansion), with parameters following the intent rather than the reverse.

**C2 · Coverage measurement — the honest version**
- **Option C**: `topOfSearchImpressionShare` + SQP brand share + `KeywordRank`. In-policy. No scraper.
- Adopt Intentwise's SOV **model** even on weaker data — the formula, the **organic-vs-paid split per keyword**, best-and-average position — so it stays comparable if the source ever changes.
- Coverage scoreboard as a **tab on Analytics**.
- Run the variation experiment: do two children of one parent ever co-occupy a SERP? Decides whether AIREON's unified parent costs coverage. *Measurement only.*

**C3 · The coverage loop on ONE family** *(an experiment, not a rollout)*
- GALE or AIREON. `lanes` for multi-placement (already modelled, unused) + the **bid ladder** across ASINs sharing the keyword set. `ads-tos-defense.job` turned on **scoped to that family alone**.
- Add the **Revive** mechanic (Scale Insights) — recover page-one presence on terms that went quiet.
- **With a control group.** Measure cost per page-one appearance **and per-ASIN conversion drift** — because the industry default is to *consolidate* keyword ownership, not spread it, and multi-ASIN coverage fragments conversion signal.

**C4 · Widen or stop** — a real decision gate on C3's numbers, including the option to conclude coverage is not worth buying at this spend.

### Stage D — Extend *(only if C4 says yes)*

**D1 · Ad-type stacking** — SB, SB Video, SD create+optimize paths (today read-only in `ads-api-client.ts`). Separate inventory pools, so they stack on one page without competing with SP bids.
**D2 · Autonomy** — promote per category to Auto within bounds; supervisor with a daily envelope and an anomaly guard that trips to Observe; morning digest as the only routine touchpoint.
**D3 · Retire legacy** — redirect `/marketing/advertising/*` and `/marketing/ads-console/*`; delete only after traffic is zero.

### Deliberately dropped from v1

- **Authority / per-field ownership + precedence resolver.** Separation replaced arbitration: the rank engine owns placement exclusively, rules keep only what it can't do. Bounds-on-entity (A1) covers the residue. This was the single largest build in v1.
- **Scraped SOV.** See Part 3.
- **AMC incrementality**, cross-retailer breadth, revenue recovery, autonomous campaign structure creation, 200+ parameters, opaque ML.

## Part 5 — Design systems: the open gap

This is the one original command not yet properly served.

**What is decided:**
- **Zero new navigation.** All ~55 orphaned controls map onto the 18 existing ads-rail entries. Confirmed feasible when the carry-over was mapped.
- DS components only from `apps/web/src/design-system`: DataGrid + GridToolbar + FilterBar (the `/products/next` stack, all four stylesheets), `Tabs`, `Drawer` with confirms through the `overlay=` slot, `MetricStrip`, `Heatmap`, `Banner`, `Toast`.
- Known traps respected: grid cards clip a dropdown's last option; menus in sticky cells must portal to `document.body`; DataGrid needs scoped `table-layout:fixed` via a plain global class; the DS guard greps comments.
- **Organising principle borrowed from Intentwise**: data → visibility → execution. Our three consoles are an accident; their three layers are a decision.

**What is missing:** your study contains `06-UI-TEARDOWN` for all five companies and **I have not read them.** Before Stage B is designed — the Control Console is the single most important surface in this plan — those teardowns should be the input. Re-deriving competitor UI patterns I could simply read would be waste.

**Recommendation:** read the five UI teardowns as the first task of Stage B, not as a separate phase.
