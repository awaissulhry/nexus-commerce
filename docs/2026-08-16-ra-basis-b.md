## 1 · What landed

**B1's recency guard, and it was not the no-op it was framed as.**

`sqpImpressionShareForAsins` returned a share from the newest SQP week with **no age check at all**,
while being the only feedback signal a Rest-of-Search rank lane has. Measured on production:

| | |
|---|---|
| campaigns evaluated | 220 |
| open-loop **before** | 98 |
| open-loop **after** | 101 |
| **changed** | **3** |

```
IT Racing Suit CE Exact · CE Broad Only · X Phrase Only
  before  0.00%   ← a week 35 DAYS old, covering 1 of 49 advertised ASINs
  after   null (open-loop)
```

🔴 **Market-level lag is not campaign-level lag.** All four markets' newest SQP week is 2026-08-02 —
14 days, comfortably inside the limit. But SQP coverage is **per-ASIN**, and those three campaigns'
ASIN sets do not appear in that week at all; their newest row is 2026-07-12. A rank-defend loop
reads 0% as *"we hold nothing, push bias up"*, so the engine was raising bias on three live
campaigns from a five-week-old zero drawn from one forty-ninth of their catalogue.

**A stale zero is indistinguishable from a real one without both the age and the basis.** That is
why the reading carries `contributors {withData, total}` and not just `ageDays` — SOV's requirement,
now enforced at the source rather than at the chip.

**The threshold is not new.** 28 days and the 0.34 thin-basis fraction are RD.P4's, measured for the
freshness label `rank-runtime` already renders; they moved into `sqp.service.ts` rather than being
re-invented. Arming at 14 would have taken every Rest-of-Search lane open-loop today — a live
bidding change no substrate session should make.

**The guard is opt-in and logged.** `sqpImpressionShareForAsins` keeps its exact behaviour for both
callers and delegates; the engine calls `sqpShareForAsins` and decides. Returning `null` on age from
inside the shared function would have changed what `rank-runtime` renders and what the engine bids,
silently, from a function neither asked to change.

## 2 · Measurements for the five unbuilt units — do not re-derive these

The brief's §2 is stale in five ways. Corrected here so the next session starts from truth.

**2.1 — Six local `EmptyState` copies, not three.** `keyword-harvest:795` · `negative-targeting:622`
· `bid:1066` · `apply-rules:1013` · `budget:1009` · `placement:1076`. Only
`budget-schedules/SectionShell.tsx` uses the DS one. The DS `EmptyState` takes
`{icon, title, description, action, className}` — it can carry all four states' **content**, but has
**no tone axis**, and §3.3's law is that "refused" must never share a colour with "broke". So the
honest move is *extend* (one `tone` prop) and say so, not adopt-as-is and not fork a seventh.

**2.2 — `bestShareForAsins` does not exist.** The function is `sqpImpressionShareForAsins`
(`sqp.service.ts`), and it has **two** callers, not one: `ad-rank-defend.job.ts:567` and
`rank-runtime.service.ts:296`.

**2.3 — 🔴 The freshness contract already exists server-side and nobody has promoted it.**
`rank-runtime.service.ts` (RD.P4) already returns all four states with age **and** volume:
`kind:'no-coverage'` (with the wording *"an onboarding problem, not a stale feed — no recency guard
would fix it"*), `kind:'no-signal'`, `freshness:'fresh'|'stale'`, `ageDays`, `staleReason`,
`contributors{withData,total}`. Its `:305` comment says it reads the age **outside**
`sqpImpressionShareForAsins` deliberately — *"this page must not change what that function
returns."* **`<FreshnessChip>` is a promotion of this shape, not a design exercise.** B1 has since
put the same shape at the source, so the chip can now read one contract end to end.

**2.4 — The three "refusal" types are not three of one thing.** `RefusalSummary`
(`automations/LimitsView.tsx:29`) and `RefusalsResp` (`bid/BidActivity.tsx:42`) are the same
gate-denial shape — `byKind: Array<{deniedAt, count}>` is byte-identical, and `RefusalsResp` is just
that plus `recent: RefusalRow[]`. But `negative-targeting/NegRecord.tsx:46`'s `Refusal` is
`{term, orders, salesCents, times}` — **protected-term** refusals, a different subject wearing the
same word. **Converge two; rename the third.** Folding all three into one `GateDecision` would merge
two unrelated facts and is the failure mode B2 exists to prevent.

**2.5 — `/advertising/changes` exists and needs `grep -a`.** It is at `advertising.routes.ts:7576`
(the 600 KB file — a plain grep misses it) and has **three siblings**: `changes.csv`,
`changes/:actionLogId/undo-preview`, `changes/:actionLogId/undo`. `/advertising/action-log` is at
`advertising-intel.routes.ts:2472`. B5 is therefore not "two routes, pick one" — it is one route
**with an undo path attached** versus one without, which is very likely outcome 2 (two grains, both
survive, named). Diff them before deciding; do not delete the one carrying `undo`.

## 3 · What the spec still gets wrong

Beyond §0.0's six, one more, from B1:

🔴 **A freshness endpoint that reports one age per SOURCE will be wrong.** §5's freshness contract
and the brief's B1 table both model freshness as `MAX(date)` per source. B1 measured that market
lag (14 d) and campaign lag (35 d) differ by three weeks on the same feed on the same day, because
SQP coverage is per-ASIN. `GET /advertising/freshness` must expose age **per scope**, or every page
that renders it will call those three campaigns fresh — reproducing the exact defect the guard just
closed, one layer up.
