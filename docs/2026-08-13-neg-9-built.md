## 1 · 🔴 The protection guards one door out of two

`protectConverting` and the write gate both bind **our** write path. A negation created at Amazon
and mirrored in by the v1 sync passes **neither**.

```
negatives with no create log — they arrived by sync   1,227 of 2,059   (59.6%)
```

That is the majority of the base, not an edge. And neither existing detector can see it: Detector A
needs an ad-group overlap; Detector B needs 30 days of silence. A converting term negated today,
outside Nexus, in a campaign that still runs it, is invisible to both.

Two negations arrived that way in the last three days — `motorradjacke 4xl` and
`veste moto homme homologué` — and neither carries an action-log row.

## 2 · The third detector

Fires when **all four** hold: blocking (NEG.4's predicate) · created inside a 14-day lookback ·
the term has ≥1 order in the window · **and no `create_negative_*` action log**.

That fourth condition is what makes it a blind-spot detector rather than a second copy of the gate.
A negation we created has already been judged by `protectConverting`; **396 recent blocking
negations we did create are correctly excluded.**

🔴 **It renders as a review queue, not an alarm.** Negating a converting term inside an AUTO campaign
is standard funnel routing — you push it to its exact campaign so it stops competing with itself.
No row is called an error, a loss or wasted spend. Each carries the campaign's targeting type (AUTO
chipped green), *"negated in N, runs in M"*, and the honest sentence: *created outside Nexus, so the
converting-term protection did not apply; check it was intended.*

## 3 · 🔴 Its zero is window-dependent, exactly as Detector A's is

| window | inbound | candidates |
|---|---|---|
| 30d | **0** | 2 |
| 60d | **1** | 2 |

`candidates` — rows eligible *before* the converting test — is on the payload for the same reason
NEG.4 carries `overlapsRelaxed`: it is what separates "checked and found nothing" from "the query
failed". The empty state says all three things: how many arrived, that none of their terms took an
order, and that the count moves with the window.

**A correction to §2 worth keeping.** The brief calls `motorradjacke 4xl` a converting term at
2 orders / €182.18. That is the **exact search term at 60 days**; inside 30 days it has **0 orders**.
And NEG.6's winning-gram table, which §2 cites, is the **2-gram over 60 days** — 4 orders / €348.56
across 8 queries. Three different numbers with one name, which is the fifth unit collision this
engagement has produced.

## 4 · 🔴 `Account-wide negative sync` — delete it, do not repair it

**The brief's diagnosis is half right.** It fails `keyword + marketplace required`, but the
marketplace **is** supplied — `triggerData` carries it on all 5,000 sampled executions. Only the
**keyword** is missing, and it is missing from the trigger's `adTarget` projection (which carries
`{ id, clicks, orders, spendCents }` and no `expressionValue`), not from the rule config.

**Reconstructed over 60 days from its own trigger history — the decision number:**

adTargets its trigger selected                26
of those, terms that CONVERTED                12   🔴
    motorrad jacke herren    16 orders  €1,391.39   → would be negated in all 8 enabled DE campaigns
    giacca moto estiva uomo   5 orders    €457.35

    giacca moto               3 orders    €253.29
    b0cxpp5dbk                3 orders    €243.45   ← an ASIN, negated as a keyword phrase

Seven of the 26 are AUTO targets whose `expressionValue` is the **empty string**. And it duplicates

**Recommendation: delete.** It is inert only by accident, and a partial repair is the dangerous
state. Nothing about it was changed.

## 7 · The defect only measuring found

🔴 **`1,227of this account's negatives`.** The source reads `<b>{...}</b> of this account's` with a
space; the built output is `<b>1,227</b>of`. The transform dropped the space following `</b>` on the
same line while the explicit `{' '}` one line above survived.

`innerText` showed "1,227of" — but this page has twice been lied to by textContent concatenation, so
the check was **geometry**: the following text node starts with "of", sits on the same line, and the
gap is **0px**. `innerHTML` then confirmed it.

Fixed at all three sites in the new block, not only the one today's data renders. The general
detector this produced — flag any element whose next text-node sibling sits on the same line with a
≤1px gap and neither side supplies its own space — found exactly one real case across all five
sections and one wrapped-line false positive.
