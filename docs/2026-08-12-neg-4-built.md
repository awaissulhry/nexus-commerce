## 1 · The hard part was the zero

Detector A's correct answer is 0, and a broken join returns 0 too. Everything structural in this
section exists to tell those apart.

**The payload carries two things that exist only to make a zero legible:**

- `conflicts.overlapsRelaxed` — the same join with the blocking filter removed. Non-zero proves the
  join works, so the zero above it is a policy result rather than a failed read. **Measured: 3.**
- `coverage.searchTermRows` — a real count of rows read. **Measured: 194.** When it is 0 the panel
  refuses to render the detectors at all and says so, because a failed read and a quiet account
  produce identical numbers and only one of them is good news.

**`_neg4-detectors.mts` asserts in two stages** for the same reason: stage 1 proves the JOIN on the
relaxed set and must be NON-ZERO; stage 2 proves the POLICY and must be zero. A stage-2 pass with a
stage-1 failure means the zeros are fake, and the script prints exactly that. The broken
external↔local join returns 0 where the correct one returns 3, so the two are distinguishable by
construction rather than by hope.

## 2 · 🔴 Two corrections, both measured

**(a) The relaxed overlap is 3, not 1.** The brief expects 1. Three terms overlap, all in ad group
`64646958871677`: `saponette moto`, `saponette per tuta moto`, `saponette tuta moto`.

The oracle's "1" is its **converting** subset (`orders > 0`), not the overlap count — its own line
reads *"converting terms whose traffic ad group is ALSO a negating ad group: 1"*. The script now
reproduces both numbers and asserts the subset relation, so this is a refinement of the oracle
rather than a disagreement with it. All six overlapping negations are ARCHIVED, which is why the
policy filter collapses 3 → 0, and each collapse is returned with its reason rather than dropped.

**(b) 🔴 Detector A's zero is a property of the WINDOW, not of the account.** The brief treats
"0 blocking conflicts" as a fact about the account. It is a fact about the last 30 days:

| window | conflicts | relaxed overlaps |
|---|---|---|
| 30d | **0** | 3 |
| 60d | 2 | 5 |
| 120d | **6** | 12 |

And the 120-day figure was **9** before a second correction the brief did not anticipate: for three
of those nine, the ad group's last impression for the term lands **on or before the day the negation
was created** — `chaqueta moto hombre` negated 2026-05-26, last impression 2026-05-26. Those are not
conflicts; they are **the negative working**. A date-blind overlap counts pre-negation traffic as
evidence that a negation blocks live traffic, which is the study's "cries wolf" failure reappearing
on the time axis instead of the grain axis.

**A conflict now requires the traffic to postdate the negation.** `createdAt` is when *we* first saw
the row, which for a mirrored negation is at or after Amazon created it — so this can only ever
exclude a real conflict, never invent one. The safe direction, and stated rather than assumed.

The window control is therefore load-bearing and on screen, and the panel says *"in the last 30
days"* rather than "nothing is wrong".

## 6 · The defect only clicking found — six sections, six defects

Under campaign `GALE | IT | Exact | Category`, whose four negations are all local-only so none of
them blocks, the panel read:

> "0 of **0** blocking negatives are in conflict in the last 30 days."

A denominator of zero means there was **nothing to check** — a different fact from "we checked and
found nothing", and framing it as a clean result is a lie by arithmetic. It is the fourth empty state
the brief asks for, and I had missed it.

It now renders in neutral grey rather than the green used for a real all-clear: *"Nothing to check
here. This scope holds no blocking negation at all — its 4 negations are archived, unconfirmed at
Amazon, or in a campaign that is not enabled — so there is nothing a conflict could be found
against. The account has 942 blocking negations elsewhere."*

The same fix corrected a second slip in that sentence: the "the check ran" clause quoted the
**account-wide** relaxed overlap count while sitting under a campaign scope, reading as though those
overlaps were in the scope you were looking at.

That is now six sections and six defects that a green pre-push and a passing script did not catch —
four in NEG.1, a fifth empty state in NEG.2, delivery honesty in NEG.3, a non-reactive URL read in
NEG.3b, and this.
