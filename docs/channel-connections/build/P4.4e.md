# P4.4e — price read-back

**Date** 2026-09-21 · **Package** P4.4 (Price), fifth and last slice · **Size** M

The plan row: *"Price read-back."*

---

## 1. What was measured

### 1.1 🔴 The cheapest finding in the package: the price was already in our hands

Both scheduled read-backs **already fetch the price and throw it away**:

| Job | The call it already makes | What it keeps | What it drops |
|---|---|---|---|
| `amazon-qty-readback` (daily, per market) | `GET_MERCHANT_LISTINGS_ALL_DATA` via `fetchActiveCatalog` | `quantity` | **`price`** — parsed at `amazon.service.ts:452` into `CatalogItem.price` |
| `shopify-qty-readback` (P4.3f, every 6 h) | `VARIANT_QUERY` — `{ id sku price product { id } inventoryItem { … } }` | `available` | **`price`** — selected by the query, dropped by the reader |

So a price read-back on both channels costs **zero additional API calls**. It is
not a new integration; it is keeping a field we already ask for.

### 1.2 Price drift was invisible on every channel

| Channel | Write-time check | Scheduled check |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify | **strong** — reads the variant back after the write and throws if it differs (`listing-write.service.ts:209`) | **none** |
| Amazon | none | **none** |
| eBay | none | **none** |

And a write-time check, as P4.2c and P4.3f both put it, **cannot detect drift
between writes**. A shop-side repricer, a promotion, a third-party app or a
silently failed push all move the price, and nothing here noticed.

Checked before building, because five of eleven rows in this package have been
counterweights: the daily `reconcile-cron` does cover drift, but it reads
`ChannelStockEvent` — **stock**, not price. No price read-back job existed.

## 2. What was built, and the one decision inside it

`services/price-readback.service.ts`: `priceDrift` (pure), `priceDriftMessage`,
and `priceHealEnabled`. Wired into **both** existing read-backs.

### 2.1 🔴 It REPORTS and does not heal

The quantity read-back beside it heals — it re-enqueues the intended quantity.
This does not, and the reason is the same one that made P4.4c refuse rather than
clamp, one step further:

> A quantity heal is bounded by the pool. **A price heal is a money write made by
> a machine on a schedule**, with nothing above it but P4.4c's floor and ceiling
> — and most products have neither.

The Owner has not ruled on automatic price correction, so the read-back raises
the conflict and stops. `NEXUS_ENABLE_PRICE_READBACK_HEAL` names the switch for
the day that ruling exists, and it is read as exactly `'true'` so no truthy
accident turns it on.

### 2.2 Money compares in cents, and "not told" is not "told zero"

`priceDrift` rounds both sides to cents before subtracting — `0.1 + 0.2 !== 0.3`
in floats, and a read-back that reports that as drift reports it forever.

A missing price on **either** side returns `null`, never a drift of that amount:
conflating *"the channel did not tell us"* with *"the channel says 0"* turns
every unpriced listing into a false conflict. But a channel price **of** zero on
a listing we intend to sell **is** reported — the price equivalent of the scoped
Zero, and zero is a value, not a gap.

### 2.3 Two details the arms do not share

- **FBA rows ARE compared for price**, unlike the quantity arm. Amazon owns FBA
  *stock*; it does not own the *price* — a merchant sets that either way, so
  excluding FBA would blind the check on the listings that sell most.
- **A PAUSED or PINNED Shopify listing is skipped for quantity but its price is
  still checked.** Those states are about what we push, not about what the shop
  is charging.

### 2.4 Its own conflict type, and its own counters

`CHANNEL_PRICE_READBACK` — not `CHANNEL_QTY_READBACK`, and not `PRICE_MISMATCH`.
A read-back conflict is *"the channel drifted from us"*, which an operator
resolves differently from a mismatch found at import. Deduped 24 h per product,
exactly as the quantity arm does it.

Both jobs report the price arm **beside** the quantity arm and never folded into
it: `… | price: compared=N mismatches=N logged=N (heal off)`. A clean quantity
run with drifted prices must not print as a clean run.

## 3. Proof

`price-readback.p44.vitest.test.ts` — **22 tests**: the verdict in cents
(including `0.1 + 0.2` vs `0.3` and a sub-cent difference); signed direction both
ways; all four "missing price" shapes returning null; a channel zero reported and
an intended zero compared; the sentence to the cent with and without a currency;
the heal switch off for `undefined` and for `'1'` and on only for `'true'`; the
Amazon arm's coordinate, its skips, and **FBA rows compared**.

`quantity-readback.p43.vitest.test.ts` gained **6 price arms**: a drift reported
with `readShopifyAvailable` called **once** for both arms; nothing healed; a
PAUSED listing skipped for quantity with its price still checked; agreement
reporting nothing; a null price not a drift to zero; the 24 h dedupe.

**Mutation checks — 9 of 9 convicted:** floats instead of cents, a missing
channel price diffed as zero, a channel zero skipped as falsy, a missing intended
price diffed as zero, the heal switch loosened to truthy, the Amazon arm skipping
FBA, the Shopify arm not running, the Shopify reader dropping the price, and the
Shopify arm healing.

🔴 **"The Shopify reader stops returning the price" passed GREEN first**, and the
reason is the banked *a fixture PINS a dimension* trap: the read-back's own test
**mocks** `listing-write.service.js`, so no arm of it could ever convict the real
reader — *the arm that would have failed is the one never run*. A third block now
exercises the real `readShopifyAvailable` against a stubbed GraphQL, and asserts
**one** call for both arms, a null price staying null, and `'0.00'` becoming `0`.

**Gates.** `tsc` clean · gateway ratchet all zeros, 0 rows outside
`outbound-rows.ts` · market-currency ✓ · **profiles-ON ratchet ✓** (839 files) ·
full suite **6 failed / 10,465 passed**, the documented baseline.

## 4. What this does NOT do

- **eBay has no price read-back.** Its read-back service reads inventory, and a
  price read would be a genuine extra call rather than a free field. It is the
  one channel where this row costs something, and it is not built.
- **Nothing is healed**, by design (§2.1). The done-when for the Owner is a
  ruling on automatic price correction.
- **No production measurement.** How much price drift exists right now is
  unknown; the first Amazon run (daily, 04:15 UTC) and Shopify run (every 6 h)
  will say. The done-when is one `CHANNEL_PRICE_READBACK` row, or a cron line
  reading `price: compared=N mismatches=0` with N > 0.
